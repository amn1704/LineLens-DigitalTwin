# Demonstrating LineLens

Run the backend on port 8102 and the frontend with Vite. Use **Reset demo** in the Dashboard viewport before each scenario; reset returns the simulator to its healthy warm baseline.

## Healthy factory

Open Dashboard, choose a station, and compare Observed, Twin, and Forecast. Use a basic-data station such as Trim Station to explain why confidence and data source are visible alongside the estimate.

## Bottleneck warning

Open **Demo scenarios** from the Dashboard viewport and choose **Bottleneck**. The local simulator generates persistent Chassis Marriage drift, then the prediction service produces the warning and computed no-intervention forecast. Open Incidents to show the evidence and recommended human checks.

## Vehicle quality

Choose **Weld quality issue** in Demo scenarios. Quality will show the risky vehicle cohort generated by the quality pipeline. Select an Inspect vehicle to explain its Digital Build Record, likely origin, recommended inspection point, and Common Pattern.

## Sensor loss

Choose **Sensor loss** and inspect Trim Station. The view makes the reduced direct evidence and confidence visible while the Twin remains an estimate rather than pretending data is available.

## Learning

Open **Help** for the complete five-minute product tour, or use **Guide this page** in a workspace for a short focused explanation.
